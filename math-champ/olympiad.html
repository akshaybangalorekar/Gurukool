🏅 The Olympiad Ladder

Real Olympiad questions, starting at Grade 5 and climbing. Every problem has three helpers, in this order:

  1. What do we know? pulls the facts out of the words - no method, just the data.
  2. Guide me step by step asks you small questions, one at a time, and checks each one.
  3. Nudges if you are stuck, and a full worked solution only after you have had a real go.

The answer is never shown before you have tried. That is the whole point.

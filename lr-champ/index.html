Teaser puzzle — try it now

Three boxes are labelled, but every label is wrong: "Pencils", "Erasers", and "Pencils and Erasers". You may pick ONE item from ONE box (without looking inside) and then must fix all three labels. Which box do you pick from?

Pick from the box labelled "Pencils and Erasers". Since its label is wrong, it contains ONLY pencils or ONLY erasers. Say you draw a pencil — then this box is the Pencils box. Now the box labelled "Erasers" is wrong too, and it can't be Pencils (taken), so it must be "Pencils and Erasers". The last box gets the remaining label. One draw, all three labels fixed — that's the power of a good deduction!
